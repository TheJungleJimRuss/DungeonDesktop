# 08: Sessions and scenes

**What to build:** In Prep mode the DM plans a Session as an ordered list of scenes, each with a goal, notes, secrets and clues, and can link scenes to locations and NPCs. During play, secrets and clues are ticked off and scenes marked played. After the game, a log is written and unused items carry over. The Dashboard shows the current Session summary.
