# 03: Search across spells, items, equipment and conditions

**What to build:** Global search covers all SRD reference types, not just monsters. Results are grouped by type, can be filtered by type (and for monsters by challenge rating and creature type), and tolerate typos and partial words. Any result opens as a panel (spell entries show level, school, casting time, range, components, duration, description; items and conditions have their own readable layouts) and can be sent to the Pop-out Window.

**Blocked by:** 02 SRD data and monster search
