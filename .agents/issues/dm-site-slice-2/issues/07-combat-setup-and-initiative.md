# 07: Combat setup and initiative

**What to build:** In Combat mode the DM starts an Encounter and adds combatants: Party members, SRD or custom monsters found by search, and ad-hoc combatants with just a name, initiative and HP. Copies of one monster are auto-numbered (Goblin 1, Goblin 2). Initiative can be rolled for all monsters at once and typed in for players; the order sorts automatically with a manual tie-break. The DM advances and rewinds turns and rounds, with the active combatant and round clearly shown. An in-progress Encounter survives a reload. The tracker is its own panel in the Combat layout.
